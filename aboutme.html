About Me

Chloe King is an artist born in Stockton California and quickly moved and grew up in Folsom California. She spent most of her after school time creating drawings and little stories. Eventually she started her practice in digital art during elementary school. She graduated from Folsom High School obtaining her diploma in 2021. After Highschool, she started gaining internet attraction in small niche communities online with her fan art. In 2024 she gotten her Associates in Studio art from Folsom Lake College and transferred to San Jose State University the same year. The following year she get accepted into SJSU's Digital Media Art program. She will parttake in her first ever group exhibtion featuring DMA students around April/May of 2027. She plans on obtaining a Bachelor's in Digital Media Art in Spring of 2027. Once she graduates, she plans and getting involved with the up and coming indie animation industry.
