Artist Statement

I am a digital media artist who specializes in character design, digital illustration and animation. I have been doing digital illustrations for over 10 years. My focus is creating fan work of preexisting media, preferably of television programs. Besides fan art, I also love to build immersive world with complex narratives using the characters I have created. My characters start as a simply concept but can grow into more complex people. If I do not design these characters for myself, I design them for others. I enjoy bringing others' ideas to life in a fully-fledged design. I do not have much collaborating experience as of now but my goal is to collaborate with as many creatives as possible. I want my work to compliment an existing project well and I want my characters to not only be aesthetically pleasing but to grow from the very idea they initially represent. I want my work to be readable to people and to not have the viewers or clients have to second guess themselves to try and 'decipher' my work. I hope I can use my skills to help up and coming creatives in the entertainment industry.
